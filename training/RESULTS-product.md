This is the number to quote. `training/RESULTS.md` scores the raw adapter on whole rows, which is a useful diagnostic and not what anyone runs: on the same pairs that path reads +0.54 BLEU and −0.79 chrF2, because feeding several sentences at once makes the model drop a clause and the app never does that.

The base model prints its language tag into the translation itself — `>>eng<<` and friends — in 572 of 2,009 outputs (28.5%). Lilly does it in 0 (0.0%). That is a defect a reader sees, so the scores are given both with it and without: with, because it is what arrives on screen; without, because otherwise the fine-tuning gets credit for translation quality it did not gain.
