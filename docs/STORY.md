# Why Lilly exists

I did not start Lilly because the world needed another demo.

I started it because people kept telling me I did not know **Bosnian**.

When I applied for an internet password. When I applied for jobs. When they ran
classes. The same line, over and over: *you do not know Bosnian — you will not
understand.*

That sentence sits in your chest. You start asking yourself if they are right.

Then I asked a different question.

**Why don’t I train real Bosnian myself?**

If they talk, why can’t I press the microphone, listen, and hear it in English?
If they write on the board, why can’t I take a photo and understand it in English?
If I need to say something, why can’t I type it and have it spoken for me?

So I stopped waiting for someone else’s model to care.

I built my own way through: **type**, **speak**, **snap** — Bosnian in, English
out, trained and measured for Bosnian on purpose. Not “South Slavic, close
enough.” Not a flag swap. The language they said I did not know.

I think that was the smart move.

Lilly is that decision, turned into software.

— [@ssaaffaakk](https://github.com/ssaaffaakk)
